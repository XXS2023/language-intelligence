PROJECT 2: LANGUAGE INTELLIGENCE

Selected sector for pilot version: HS85 — Electrical machinery and electronics
Time window: 2022-01-01 to 2025-09-30
Pilot corpus target: 20 Chinese articles + 20 English articles

1) SOURCE LIST

CHINESE SOURCES
1. Xinhua Finance / 新华财经
   - Strong for official framing and economic policy language
   - Good for China-side policy and supply-chain narratives

2. China Daily (business and policy coverage)
   - Useful for government-aligned narrative framing
   - Good for strategic industrial discourse

3. People’s Daily / 人民网财经
   - Useful for policy and state-communication framing
   - Good for industrial and national security narrative vocabulary

4. Ministry of Commerce (MOFCOM / 商务部)
   - Excellent for official trade and industrial policy vocabulary
   - Important for import/export dependency framing

5. General Administration of Customs (GACC / 海关总署)
   - Good for trade flow and customs language
   - Useful for imports, exports, equipment, and categories

6. First Financial / 第一财经 (if access is feasible)
   - Good for business-news framing and market tone

7. Caixin / 财新 (if access is feasible)
   - Useful for corporate and industrial commentary

ENGLISH SOURCES
1. Reuters
   - Best for balanced, high-signal business and trade coverage
   - Very useful for comparison with Chinese descriptions

2. Financial Times
   - Strong on supply chain, industrial strategy, and market interpretation

3. Euractiv / POLITICO Europe
   - Useful for policy and EU-level framing

4. European Commission trade / industry pages
   - Strong for official EU policy tone and terminology

5. OECD / WTO / World Bank pages
   - Good for cross-border trade and dependency framing

6. Bloomberg or similar high-quality business source (if accessible)
   - Useful for market-scale and strategic-risk language

7. Major Polish English-language business coverage (optional)
   - Useful for Poland-centered reporting, if available

SOURCE RULES
- Keep a balanced mix of official, business, and policy sources
- Keep no single source above ~35% of one language corpus
- Keep the same time window across languages
- Use same sector filter across both corpora
